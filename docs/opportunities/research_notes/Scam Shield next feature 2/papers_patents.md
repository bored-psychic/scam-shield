### Training vs moment-of-risk warnings
- Ho et al., IEEE S&P 2025, UC San Diego Health: 8-month RCT, 10 simulated campaigns, ~19,500 staff; no significant link between recent annual training and failing; embedded training ~2% lower click likelihood; lure mattered (1.8% Outlook password lure vs 30.8% vacation-policy lure) - [PDF](https://sysnet.ucsd.edu/~voelker/pubs/phishtrain-oakland25.pdf), [UCSD news](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/cybersecurity-training-programs-dont-prevent-employees-from-falling-for-phishing-scams). Check exact numbers in PDF.
- Reproduction: "Anti-Phishing Training (Still) Does Not Work", WWW 2026 - [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3774904.3792467)
- Lain, Kostiainen, Capkun, IEEE S&P 2022: 15 months, >14,000 employees; warnings in emails effective; embedded training did not help, sometimes made employees more susceptible; crowd reporting works - [arXiv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2112.07498). Follow-up arXiv 2409.01378: effect from periodic reminders not content - [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.01378)
- Vance, Jenkins, Anderson, Bjornn, Kirwan, MIS Quarterly 2018: habituation (fMRI + eye tracking + 3-week field on app permission warnings); polymorphic warnings slow adherence decline - [MISQ](https://misq.org/tuning-out-security-warnings-a-longitudinal-examination-of-habituation-through-fmri-eye-tracking-and-field-experiments.html)
- Payment warnings: CeDEx Nottingham discussion paper 2023-08 (online experiment in mock banking app: redesigned calls-to-action reduce fraud success strongly; traditional warnings weak) - [PDF](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/cedex/documents/papers/cedex-discussion-paper-2023-08.pdf). OBIE RCT >13k participants on APP-fraud on-screen interventions - [OBIE](https://www.openbanking.org.uk/event/using-behavioral-insights-and-experimentation-to-prevent-app-fraud/) (results to check)
- Confirmation of Payee: PwC Strategy&/OeNB/Bundesbank/EBA 2023 study: UK banks with CoP APP fraud -10% on average vs +60% at banks without; Dutch bank invoice fraud fell but migrated cross-border; "IBAN-name checks alone cannot significantly drive down fraud" - [OeNB](https://www.oenb.at/en/Media/Press-Archives/2023/20230606.html)
- Cooling-off: Singapore 24-h cooling when half of balance rapidly drained (MAS 2025) - [MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/letters-to-editor/2025/joint-response-to-two-letters-on-delays-in-banks-processing-local-fund-transfers); UK up to 4 business days delay with reasonable grounds (2024 regs) - [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/app-fraud-uk-government-publishes-draft-legislation-allowing-psps-to-delay-payments-for-fraud-concerns); no outcome evaluation found yet. Korea delayed withdrawal (30 min, designer says "not a fundamental solution" but gives "golden time") - [JoongAng](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/inside-the-intricate-world-of-voice-phishing/10840399)
- Australia: 2024 reported losses -26% to ~A$2.0bn, reports -17.8% (494,732); banks credit Scam-Safe Accord (CoP, warnings, payment delays, crypto payment stops); no independent attribution - [BNA](https://www.businessnewsaustralia.com/articles/banks-declare-they-are-playing-their-part-as-losses-to-scams-fall-by---700m-over-the-past-year.html), [ABA](https://www.ausbanking.org.au/banks-welcome-more-progress-in-scams-war-as-losses-fall)

### Primary-source checks (read in full text) - added 04:45
- Ho et al. S&P 2025 (read PDF pp.1-6): 19,789 active full-time staff after cleaning; 5 arms (~3,950 each: control 404 page, generic static, generic interactive, contextual static, contextual interactive) x 4 tracks; Jan-Oct 2023; failure rate by lure 1.82% (Outlook pwd) to 30.80% (vacation policy); 56% (11,077/19,789) clicked at least once over 8 months; month 1 9.7%. Key quotes: "embedded training provides a statistically-significant reduction in average failure rate, but of only 2%"; "over half of all training sessions end within 10 seconds and less than 24% of users formally complete the training materials"; interactive training "can reduce the likelihood of clicking on a subsequent phishing lure by 19%" (relative, only among completers). - [PDF](https://sysnet.ucsd.edu/~voelker/pubs/phishtrain-oakland25.pdf)
- Jensen, Gerlings, Ferwerda, "Do Awareness Campaigns Reduce Financial Fraud?", European Journal on Criminal Policy and Research 2024 (read full text): Spar Nord (Danish bank) mass warning in online banking portal, Sept 2020, clients over 40; 1,447 observations (all known fraud victims 6 months before/after + 1,200 sampled baseline clients); DiD + fuzzy RDD at age 40: "None of our models ... find any evidence that the campaign had a significant effect"; after the message there was "a disproportionately large jump in the proportion of fraud victims over 40". Also reviews: AARP 2003 peer-counselling RCT (119 at-risk people from seized call sheets; warning call reduced susceptibility to a mock pitch 5 days later), Scheibe et al. 2014 (895 past victims; forewarning reduced susceptibility at 2-4 weeks), Burke et al. 2022 JEBO (2,600 panelists, short video/text reduced investment-fraud susceptibility) - [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10610-024-09573-1)
- Sharevski, Vander Loop, Evans, Ponticello, "(Blind) Users Really Do Heed Aural Telephone Scam Warnings", IEEE S&P 2025 (read results): 36 legally blind + 36 sighted US participants, naturalistic cold calls (robocall interest-rate and Social Security "press one" pitches), baseline / short / contextual aural warning, 6 per group per cell. Only 2 of 72 "pressed one" (both blind, contextual warning; one accidental screen-reader slip, one deliberate to waste the scammer's time). BUT in the baseline (no warning) condition nobody pressed one either: sighted participants mostly let calls go to voicemail; cues were unknown number, "Scam Likely" STIR/SHAKEN label, synthetic voice, area code. So the study shows warnings are understood and accepted, not that they change behaviour vs no warning. - [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04014)
- Shen et al., "'It Warned Me Just at the Right Moment': Exploring LLM-based Real-time Detection of Phone Scams", CHI EA 2025 (Hong Kong): despite the title, NO user study; offline turn-by-turn evaluation of 5 LLMs on Chinese transcripts (real scam videos + synthetic); real-time recall 0.98-1.00; synthetic precision ~0.70-0.77 (rises with an UNCERTAIN option); user study left to future work. - [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.03964)
- Lain, Kostiainen, Capkun, IEEE S&P 2022 (read ar5iv full text): 14,733 employees, 15 months. Warnings on suspicious emails: clicks 3,964 (no warning) vs 1,427 (short) vs 1,289 (detailed); dangerous actions 2,994 vs 998 vs 893 (about -64% to -70%); "no significant difference between short and detailed warnings". Embedded training: clicks 3,087 (no training) vs 3,593 (training), dangerous actions 2,155 vs 2,730 (training worse); 43% said the training page "made me feel safe". Crowd reporting: ~29% of employees reported at least one email; ~10% of reports within 5 min; 68% report accuracy; ~1.5 emails/day needing manual handling. - [ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2112.07498)
- Akesson, Gathergood, Quispe-Torreblanca, "Preventing Payments Fraud in the FinTech Era", CeDEx DP 2023-08 (read full text): online randomised experiment, 15,888 UK adults (Panelbase, Prolific), mock bank app, 8 payment journeys, incentivised. Control: 22% made the fraudulent payment, 57% completed legitimate ones. 'Risk-based + CTA' (warn only on risky payments + salient buttons to cancel / save for later / call the bank): -18 pp (about -81% relative; 81-85% across scenarios; laptop scenario 33% -> 5%). Behavioural warnings (loss aversion / social norms): -18% relative and "decayed with increasing exposure ... participants became desensitized"; CTA effect held (-94% by the third scenario). Cost: CTAs cut legitimate payments by ~14 pp (up to 20 pp); risk-based targeting alone raised legitimate payments +8 pp. Lab, not field. - [PDF](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/cedex/documents/papers/cedex-discussion-paper-2023-08.pdf), [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4532757)
- Parihar, Harjani, Mathur, Goldberg (Jigsaw), van der Linden, Roozenbeek, "Countering Misinformation in India through Prebunking" (PsyArXiv 2024, RSOS manuscript; read full text): 5 humorous influencer videos (spoofing, decontextualisation, perfect-solution fallacy, emotional manipulation, scapegoating); online pilot N=547 (spoofing d=.32), in-person field UP N=4,272 (spoofing d=.23; decontextualisation and scapegoating BACKFIRED, d=.17 and .38 in the wrong direction), Kerala N=1,676 (scapegoating backfire d=.29), YouTube ads to 68.3M users / 24.1M views, quiz N=129,710: significant effects on a minority of items (2/15 Hindi); focus groups N=33; RCT N=827. Authors: "limited support ... no consistent improvement in technique discernment". Funded by Google Jigsaw. Misinformation, not scams, but "spoofing" is the closest scam-relevant technique. - [OSF](https://osf.io/g2y9z/)
- Wen, Xu, Wang, Gao, Shi, Zhao (Zhejiang Univ. + Ant Group), "Mental States: A Key Point in Scam Compliance and Warning Compliance in Real Life", IJERPH 2022 (read full text): Study 1 interviews with 17 victims (age 18-25, 15 female; losses > CNY 100): 52.94% saw a payment-platform warning, 35.29% skipped it altogether, 29.41% doubted it; 11.76% "asked the scammer" about it and 23.53% self-rationalised it; victims thought "it was a general warning not targeted" at them. Study 2: 2-month field A/B test on Alipay's Intelligent Risk Control Engine (built with China's Ministry of Public Security) for transactions flagged as likely fraud: redesigned warning 1 (N=43,015) and warning 2 (N=153,470) vs the existing "Beware of Fraud" warning (~4 million users). Fraud case rate 0.24 (baseline) -> 0.13 (warning 1) and 0.17 (warning 2), chi-square p<0.01 (paper reports "62.3%" and "45.8%" improvements, which do not match the raw rates: (0.24-0.13)/0.24 = 45.8%; treat with care). Pass rate (users who continued the payment) 90.36% / 90.86% vs 94.58%. Design: first line says "The current transaction is highly likely to be deceptive and is suspected to be a telecom fraud", names the trick, states the loss. - [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/1660-4601/19/14/8294)
