# International anti-scam interventions that act at the moment of risk, and one-student Indian analogs

Status: IN PROGRESS (research notes; saved early, updated as work proceeds). Date of research: 2026-10-22.

Evidence labels used below: [OFFICIAL] government / regulator / police data; [PEER] peer-reviewed or academic; [PRESS] journalism; [VENDOR] company claims / marketing; [LAW] law-firm summaries.

## Singapore: ScamShield Suite, 1799, Money Lock, Protection from Scams Act, Project A.S.T.R.O., bank friction

### Takeaway
Singapore's scam losses fell 17.9% in 2025 (S$1,112.4m -> S$913.1m) and another 17.9% in H1 2026, but the government itself only says the drop "suggests cautiously" that the measures work; the single largest *measured* moment-of-risk saving is not an app but Project A.S.T.R.O. (police + banks text people who are mid-scam: S$267.5m averted in 2025), followed by police/bank human interventions (S$72.2m). The citizen-facing tools (ScamShield app 1.61m downloads, 1799 helpline ~500 calls/day, 84% "is this a scam?") have large usage but no published losses-averted figure, and the coercion backstop (Protection from Scams Act restriction orders) has been used only 18 times in a year, for victims who "remained deeply entrenched despite Police engagement".

### Cited Findings
Overall trend (official, primary PDFs read):
- [OFFICIAL] 2025: scam cases fell 27.6% to 37,308 (from 51,501 in 2024); losses fell 17.9% to about S$913.1m (from about S$1,112.4m). Police wording: the decrease "suggests cautiously that the anti-scam strategies and public education measures ... have made it more challenging for scammers to succeed." Median loss per case S$1,644; 67.1% of cases lost < S$5,000; 5.2% lost >= S$100,000. — [SPF Annual Scam and Cybercrime Brief 2025 (PDF, published Feb 2026)](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] 81.8% of reported scams in 2025 involved **self-effected transfers** ("scammers did not gain direct control of the victims' accounts but manipulated them into performing monetary transactions"); H1 2026: 80.8% (H1 2025: 78.8%). — [SPF ASCB 2025](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf); [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026 (PDF)](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] H1 2026: scam cases -14.4% (19,644 -> 16,821), losses -17.9% to about S$410.6m; cases with losses >= S$100,000 -24.5%. — [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf)

Government-official impersonation (closest analog to India's "digital arrest"):
- [OFFICIAL] 2025: 3,363 cases (+123.6% from 1,504) and about S$242.9m lost (+60.5% from S$151.3m), second-highest loss of any type. 34.0% of victims aged 65+; phone calls and WhatsApp the main channels. 91.7% of cases: fake bank / financial-institution caller cites suspicious card or insurance transactions; "When victims deny involvement, they are transferred to fake government officials who accuse them of money laundering. Scammers then instruct victims to transfer money to 'safety accounts' or hand over cash". 8.3%: fake ICA / airline / telco caller hands over to fake "China Police". — [SPF ASCB 2025, Annex D](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] H1 2026: losses to this type fell 31.7% to S$90.8m (from S$132.9m in H1 2025); continuing trend of ATM cash deposits and physical handover of cash and valuables. — [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] Caller-verification responses: "major retail banks have progressively introduced notifications through their bank apps, triggered at the start of outbound calls to customers, so that customers can verify whether an incoming call claiming to be from the bank is legitimate"; SPF to pilot "a single, easily recognisable prefix number which SPF officers will use to call members of the public" later in 2026; government "will also consider tagging calls from Government agencies with recognisable caller names". No outcome data yet. — [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] Single SMS sender ID "gov.sg": 289m SMS sent, 93% of the public recognise it, "zero scam SMSes sent from the gov.sg ID". Telcos let subscribers block all incoming international calls / SMS: over 1.1m subscribers block overseas calls, 270,000 block overseas SMS. — [SPF ASCB 2025, Annex G](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] National Simulated Scams Exercise (pilot 1 Mar - 31 Aug 2026): robo-callers simulating real government-impersonation scripts; "Close to about 5,000 people have participated". No outcome measure published. — [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf)

Moment-of-risk interventions with a measured "averted" number:
- [OFFICIAL] **Project A.S.T.R.O.** (Automation of Scam-fighting Tactics & Reaching Out): ASCom and partner banks share data to identify people currently paying scammers and SMS them. 2025: "six operations, sending over 32,800 SMSes to alert more than 26,000 victims. This proactive victim-centric approach averted over $267.5 million of potential losses." "Many victims remained unaware of the deception until receiving the SMS alerts, prompting them to halt further transfers." H1 2026: 3 operations, 11,700 SMS, 9,180 victims, over S$99.5m averted. — [SPF ASCB 2025](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf); [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf). Caveat: "averted" is the police's own estimate of potential further losses; method not published.
- [OFFICIAL] Joint ASCom + Community Policing Unit interventions with people whose banks flagged suspicious transfers: "In 2025, 1,266 such interventions were conducted, averting more than $72.2 million of potential losses." Total averted 2025 (all channels, incl. crypto): at least S$348m; recovered (frozen before dissipation): S$140.5m. — [SPF ASCB 2025](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] **Protection from Scams Act 2025** (in force 1 July 2025; police order banks to restrict a person's banking for up to 30 days, extendable up to 5 times): "As of 15 February 2026, 12 Restriction Orders (ROs) have been issued. Of these, one had been lifted as the individual was assessed to no longer be at risk of transferring money to the scammer, two had ceased as they had reached the statutory maximum of five extensions, while nine remain in effect." 10 of 12 asked to withdraw money for daily expenses (approved). Banks said graduated restrictions need "significant system changes", so not prioritised. — [MHA written parliamentary reply, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/restriction-orders-issued-under-protection-from-scams-act-2025/). By 30 June 2026: 18 ROs, issued for "scam victims who remained deeply entrenched despite Police engagement". — [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf). No published figure on money saved by ROs.

Self-protection tools (usage, not losses averted):
- [OFFICIAL] **Money Lock** (lock part of savings so it cannot be moved digitally): as at 31 Dec 2025 "at least 479,000 customers have locked up close to $44 billion"; as at 30 June 2026 "about 589,000 customers have locked up close to S$49 billion" (March 2024: 78,000 accounts / S$6.6bn; 44% of users aged 50+). It protects against account takeover, not against a coerced victim who unlocks or goes to a branch; no losses-averted figure. — [SPF ASCB 2025](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf); [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf); [MAS oral reply, 2024](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/2024/oral-reply-to-parliamentary-question-on-money-lock)
- [OFFICIAL] **ScamShield app / helpline 1799** (as of mid-2026): "over 1.61 million downloads, 2.88 million user checks and 1.04 million user reports. The 24/7 ScamShield helpline has received over 310,000 calls since its launch and currently receives around 500 calls daily. Of the calls received, around 84% were enquiries checking if something was a scam". "The app alerts users to over 1 million scams per year, with 76,000 users checking for scams each month and over 100,000 Android users alerted to at least one scam call or message each month." End-2025: 1.53m downloads; "A total of 439,480 activated users have used the 'Check for Scams' or 'Report a Scam' feature at least once". — [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf); [SPF ASCB 2025](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf). Singapore's population is about 6m, so ~1.6m downloads is high reach, yet only ~76,000 people a month actively check.
- [OFFICIAL] "I can ACT against Scams" campaign: ~7 in 10 surveyed saw it; 82% "were motivated to adopt anti-scam behaviours" (self-reported motivation, not behaviour). — [SPF ASCB 2025](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] **CPF Trusted Contact notification service** (2 Feb 2026): members appoint up to two trusted people who get copies of notifications for important transactions ("an extra pair of eyes"); **CPF Safety Switch** for 55+ freezes CPF online access and withdrawals. No outcome data yet. — [SPF Mid-Year Brief 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Media-Room/Statistics/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026/Mid-Year-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2026.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] Platform rules: after Meta applied ID verification to Facebook advertisers, "scam cases on Facebook decreased by about 38% from July to September 2025 compared to the same period in 2024". — [SPF ASCB 2025](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] Bank friction: MAS and major banks added friction (Oct 2025) for transactions draining large sums from high-balance accounts; cooling periods for raising limits / changing contact details; MAS concedes these "could impact legitimate" customers. No effect size published. — [SPF ASCB 2025, Annex G](https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Advisories/Scams/Annual-Scam-and-Cybercrime-Brief-2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- Rupees-saved terms: A.S.T.R.O. moves *reach at the moment of risk* (it finds victims from bank flows, so adoption is not needed) and *chance the warning changes behaviour* (a message from the police naming the transfer). It needs bank + police data; a student cannot build the detection side. The student-buildable part is the *message itself* and the *verify-by-calling path* (a 1799-like "call a human" or "check with a trusted person" step).
- The coercion backstop is tiny in volume (18 ROs in 12 months vs 3,363 impersonation cases in 2025): most people stop once a credible authority reaches them; a small hard core does not, and only legal compulsion stops them.
- Self-check tools have reach but low *active* use: ~76,000 checks a month from 1.61m downloads; 84% of 1799 calls are "is this a scam?" questions, i.e. people do pause to verify when there is a cheap human channel.
- Confounders: SPF's case counts depend on reporting; 2025 also saw Meta ad verification, SIM limits and mule restrictions, so no single tool can be credited for the fall.

### Gaps
- No published losses-averted figure for the ScamShield app, bot or 1799 helpline, Money Lock or the Shared Responsibility Framework (not checked in detail; it governs phishing-loss liability, not coerced transfers).
- No evaluation of bank "call verification" notifications or the single police caller prefix yet (pilot due late 2026).

## Hong Kong: Scameter / Scameter+, payment-time alerts fed by Scameter, Anti-Scam Helpline 18222

### Takeaway
Hong Kong is the clearest case of a *public lookup database* turned into a *payment-time warning*: Scameter (a website + app anyone can search) feeds banks' "Suspicious Account Alert" on FPS, internet banking, branch and ATM transfers. Volume is large (655,000 alerts by mid-2024), but an HKMA deputy chief executive said 20-30% of FPS users went ahead anyway (2024, press report). Police credit bank-police "upstream" interventions with "nearly half" of the 2025 fall in losses, and credit Scameter+ alerts with a 60% fall in phishing cases.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] 2025: deception cases 43,212 (-2.9%, first fall since 2019); losses fell ~11.3% from ~HK$9.2bn to ~HK$8.1bn. Online investment fraud 5,135 cases (+30.7%), HK$3.58bn lost (44.1% of all losses); 80.4% of investment victims contacted via social media / IM. Telephone deception 8,621 cases (-6.3%); "Guess Who" calls to the elderly: cases +58.1%, losses +52.8%. — [HK Government press release (Police review of 2025), 11 Feb 2026](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202602/11/P2026021100763.htm)
- [OFFICIAL] **Upstream Scam Intervention** (police + banks): 4,060 deception cases intervened in 2025 (+69.4%); intercepted payments HK$480m (+158.4%); police say these interventions account for "nearly half of the decline" in total losses. — [HK Government press release, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202602/11/P2026021100763.htm)
- [OFFICIAL] **Scameter+**: 1.15m downloads by end-2025 (HK population ~7.5m); police say the "penetration rate still leaves room for improvement". After October 2025 upgrades (automatic database updates, SMS / IM reporting), public reports of suspicious numbers rose ~60% and websites ~50%; searches +17.6%, alerts +66.5% (over 130,000 alerts in December 2025 alone); the app proactively blocked 267 scam domains nobody had reported. Police claim the Scameter+ alert measures "led to a significant drop in phishing scams (-60%)" (phishing 1,093 cases, -60%). — [HK Government press release, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202602/11/P2026021100763.htm). Earlier: ~6.95m searches and 880,000 alerts by end-2024; >7.6m searches / ~950,000 alerts by Feb 2025 (search summary of [info.gov.hk, 11 Feb 2025](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202502/11/P2025021100473.htm); not re-read). Confounder: phishing also fell with SMS sender registration and link blocking, so the 60% is not cleanly attributable.
- [OFFICIAL] **Suspicious Account Alert** (HKMA + police + banks; launched Nov 2023 for FPS proxy IDs, extended Aug 2024 to internet banking / branch transfers to account numbers, Dec 2024 to ATMs at 22 banks): "As of end-June 2024, over 655 000 alerts were issued, with an average of 3 000 alerts per day"; after the Aug 2024 expansion coverage of bank transfers rose "from 35 per cent to 84 per cent". Q3 2024: "banks issued over 24,000 alerts" for the newly covered channels. Payees are identified from Scameter. — [HKMA press release, 1 Aug 2024](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2024/08/20240801-5/); [HKMA press release, 5 Dec 2024](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2024/12/20241205-4/)
- [PRESS] HKMA deputy chief executive Arthur Yuen said "between 20 and 30 per cent of FPS users had continued with transactions despite receiving scam alerts"; 505,000 alerts were sent between the Nov 2023 launch and March 2024, involving potential losses of about HK$600m. "We are not sure about the circumstances, we and police are looking into that." — [South China Morning Post, 2024](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/law-and-crime/article/3258557/scam-alerts-ignored-30-users-hong-kongs-faster-payment-system-bank-transfers) (paywalled; quotes taken from search-result excerpts, not read in full). No official figure on transfers actually cancelled or money saved by these alerts was found.
- [OFFICIAL, older, 2020] ADCC / Anti-Scam Helpline 18222, first three years (July 2017 - July 2020): 65,824 calls; "quashed 679 scams in progress" by persuading would-be victims to stop paying; intercepted HK$6.33bn across 1,586 scams. Method: officers give callers "spoilers" of what they think is happening and guide them to fact-check; bank staff who suspect fraud call ADCC, which sends officers to the bank. — [HK Police Offbeat, issue 1164, Jul-Aug 2020](https://www.police.gov.hk/offbeat/1164/eng/9022.html). No 2025 helpline volume found.

### Inferences
- The HK data show both halves of the user's suspicion (b): the lookup database is valuable mainly because it is *pushed into the payment screen* (alerts at 3,000/day) rather than waiting for people to search it; but 20-30% still proceed through a generic payment-time alert, so placement alone is not enough for the most committed (coerced or investment) victims.
- A Scameter-like public "is this number / account / link reported?" lookup is the most transferable government piece; India's nearest equivalents are the Sanchar Saathi "Chakshu" reporting portal and the I4C suspect registry, but (outside this note's scope) there is no public, searchable, bank-integrated version comparable to Scameter.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 Anti-Scam Helpline 18222 call / interception volumes found; no published share of alerted payments cancelled.
- No independent evaluation of Scameter's effect on losses.

## Taiwan: 165 hotline / dashboard, bank-teller "care questioning", Whoscall, Cofacts

### Takeaway
Taiwan's strongest measured moment-of-risk intervention is human: bank and post-office staff questioning customers at the counter (and calling police) stopped 14,264 cases worth NT$11.2bn in 2025, rising every year since 2023, compared with NT$89.3bn of reported losses in 2025. Monthly losses fell sharply after the July 2024 anti-fraud laws (Q4 2025 losses -50% year on year). Whoscall (vendor) saw a 37% fall in scam calls / texts in 2025. Cofacts proves a forward-to-check chatbot gets used, but its numbers are old and it targets misinformation, not payment scams.

### Cited Findings
- [PRESS citing police 165 dashboard] 2025: reported fraud losses NT$89.326bn, about 162,000 cases. Dashboard started August 2024; Aug-Dec 2025 losses NT$32.77bn vs NT$62.93bn in Aug-Dec 2024 (about -48%); Q4 2025 cases 41,526 vs 54,120 (-23%) and losses NT$18.702bn vs NT$37.2bn (-50%) per the Criminal Investigation Bureau; December 2024 NT$12.44bn vs December 2025 NT$6.667bn. — [Business Today (今周刊), 6 Jan 2026](https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/183008/post/202601060042/); [Bnext / TVBS English summaries](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/english/2755633) (secondary; dashboard itself not opened)
- [OFFICIAL via PRESS] Premier Cho Jung-tai (Aug 2025): monthly losses fell 37.7%, NT$13.11bn (Aug 2024) -> NT$8.14bn (Jul 2025); monthly cases -22.5%, 19,590 -> 15,177. — [TVBS English summary of the Premier's report](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/english/2755633) (secondary)
- [PRESS citing FSC] **Bank / financial staff counter interventions** ("臨櫃關懷提問", staff ask care questions and alert police): "金融從業人員 2025 年攔阻件數 14264 件，攔阻金額新台幣 112 億元" (14,264 cases, NT$11.2bn intercepted in 2025); "近 3 年已攔阻超過 290 億元" (over NT$29bn in 3 years). Reported 2024: 13,580 cases, over NT$10.1bn; 2023: 11,300 cases, nearly NT$7.6bn (2024/2023 from search summary of the same press family; not individually verified). — [UAnalyze / cnyes (鉅亨網), 26 Mar 2026](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/5426749667). Older FSC figure: Jan 2017 - Nov 2022, 17,119 cases / NT$7.918bn (search summary of [MoneyWeekly, Jan 2023](https://www.moneyweekly.com.tw/_Article?AID=96593)). So annual interceptions roughly grew from ~NT$1.5bn/yr (2017-22 average) to NT$11.2bn (2025). Example bank: Mega Bank 339 cases / NT$345m in 2025, credited to "主動關懷、細心詢問及即時判斷" (proactive care, careful questioning, immediate judgement). — [Commercial Times (工商時報), 19 Jan 2026](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20260119700132-430301)
- [PRESS citing FSC] Fraudulent ad takedowns: 34,679 in 2025, 50,549 in 2024. — [UAnalyze / cnyes, 26 Mar 2026](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/5426749667)
- [VENDOR] Whoscall (Gogolook) 2025 annual report (24 Feb 2026): ~25 million scam calls and texts in Taiwan in 2025, a 36.97% drop from 2024, "the most significant decrease in Asia"; Gogolook credits government anti-fraud policy and public-private cooperation; globally 480m of 6bn+ unknown calls / SMS identified as scams; Hong Kong -27% to 4.23m. — [Focus Taiwan (CNA), 24 Feb 2026](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202602240024) (paywalled; figures in the open excerpt), [GASA summary](https://gasa.org/knowledge-base/blog/gasa-and-whoscall-release-2026-asia-scam-report-taiwan). This counts scam *attempts seen by the app*, not losses prevented.
- [PRESS / NGO, older] **Cofacts** (g0v volunteers' LINE fact-check bot; users forward a message, volunteers answer, repeats are answered automatically from the database): 2018: over 46,000 messages in 3 months, 35,180 answered automatically; by 2022: over 87,000 reported messages, over 2,000 contributors; "over 57,000 interacted with its LINE bot" in one month. — [Nieman Lab, 2018](https://www.niemanlab.org/?p=162263); [Taiwan Insight, 12 Oct 2022](https://taiwaninsight.org/2022/10/12/the-bot-fighting-disinformation-the-story-of-cofacts/). No 2024-2026 numbers found and no measured effect on scam losses.
- [PRESS] Hong Kong ADCC and Whoscall launched an "anti-scam verified call" feature (May 2026) so police calls show as verified in Whoscall (caller verification). — [unwire.hk, 20 May 2026](https://unwire.hk/2026/05/20/whoscall-adcc-anti-scam-verified-call-hk/tech-secure/) (headline only; not read)

### Inferences
- The teller channel scales with training and police backing, not with software: NT$11.2bn intercepted in one year is about one eighth of the year's reported losses. Its weakness is that it only catches victims who go to a counter; app transfers bypass it (hence the shift to ATM / app friction elsewhere).
- Cofacts is the existence proof that a volunteer-run forward-to-check bot inside the dominant messenger gets heavy use with no marketing budget; the Indian analog is a WhatsApp / Telegram bot, but its value is for *pre-contact* messages, not a live coercive call.

### Gaps
- Could not open the FSC's own release for the 2025 figure; could not find what share of counter-questioned customers still insisted on paying.
- No usage numbers for the 165 hotline itself in 2025.

## South Korea: prosecutor / institution-impersonation voice phishing ("self-confinement"), telco AI call detection, payment-stop and delayed-withdrawal rules

### Takeaway
Korea is the closest structural match to India's "digital arrest": institution-impersonation calls (fake prosecutors / FSS / police) were 78.6% of 2025 voice-phishing losses, victims are told to buy a second phone, use Telegram/Signal, report hourly and isolate themselves in a motel ("셀프감금", self-confinement), and 52% of these victims are in their 20s-30s. Despite a decade of bank rules (payment-stop, 30-minute delayed withdrawal) losses hit a record 1.2578 trillion won in 2025 (+47.2%). Telcos now run AI call detection with large self-reported "prevented" figures (no independent audit); the government credits a new integrated response team, crackdowns and strikes on Cambodian call centres for a 22-25% fall from Oct 2025 to Jan 2026.

### Cited Findings
- [PRESS citing National Police Agency data via lawmaker Kwon Young-jin] Voice phishing cases / losses: 2021 30,982 / 774.4bn won; 2022 21,832 / 543.8bn; 2023 18,902 / 447.2bn; 2024 20,839 / 854.5bn; **2025 23,360 / 1,257.8bn won** (+47.2% on 2024). Institution-impersonation cases rose from 7,017 (2021) to 13,323 (2025); losses from 174.1bn to 988.4bn won, **78.6% of 2025 losses**; loan-fraud losses fell from 600.3bn to 269.4bn. Average loss per case about 25m won (2021) -> about 53.84m won (2025). — [inews24, 23 Sep 2026](https://www.inews24.com/view/2008688)
- [PRESS] Police: losses passed 1 trillion won in the first 10 months of 2025 (1,056.6bn), first time ever; police attribute higher losses per case to "sophisticated phishing tactics that psychologically manipulate victims", which delay victims' realisation. — [Korea Herald, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10631462)
- [PRESS] FSS's narrower figure (only losses filed through formal refund claims, i.e. traceable in bank accounts): 433.8bn won in 2025 (+14.1%); share of stolen funds recovered fell from 48.5% (2020) to 26.3% (2025) (secondary explainer citing FSS; not verified at source). — [seoulz.com explainer, 2026](https://www.seoulz.com/korea-voice-phishing-2026/) (low-grade secondary; treat as indicative)
- [PRESS citing police] **Self-confinement method** (Jan-Aug 2025): institution-impersonation losses 675.3bn won = 76.2% of 885.6bn total; average loss 74.38m won per case (+76.3% year on year); 20s-30s = 52% of victims. Steps: fake card / registered-mail delivery call -> victim enters details on a site that produces a fake arrest warrant in their name -> told to switch to a "security messenger" (Signal, Telegram) and report every hour -> told to buy an old second phone on which malicious apps spoof outgoing numbers and track location -> told to stay alone in lodging ("셀프감금"). Police distributed 50,000 posters to lodging facilities and planned on-site training for bank and telecom staff. — [Boannews, 18 Sep 2025](https://www.boannews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=139339)
- [PRESS citing local police] Motel / lodging interventions: Anyang Manan police found 11 self-confinement cases (420m won) in 3 months in a motel district and put prevention posters in motels with owners' help; one office worker in his 30s escaped after reading the police notice in his motel room. Jeonbuk police: a patrol found a woman in her 20s at a motel check-out counter about to check in on a fake prosecutor's instruction; another victim was found after 4 days in a motel just before handing over 50m won in withdrawn loan money; police visited 58 lodging businesses. Scammers then shifted victims to room cafes / officetels. — search summary of [Jeonbuk Ilbo (jjan.kr), 27 Aug 2025](https://www.jjan.kr/articleAmp/20250827580187), [jjan.kr, 21 Aug 2025](https://www.jjan.kr/articleAmp/20250821580215), [Daum / Chosun "조선피싱실록", 12 Oct 2025](https://v.daum.net/v/20251012050148543) (anecdotal, not read in full)
- [OFFICIAL via PRESS] Government (Office for Government Policy Coordination): cases and losses fell four months in a row from Oct 2025 to Jan 2026: cases 8,145 -> 6,108 (-25%), losses 451.8bn -> 350.8bn won (-22.4%) (comparison periods not clearly stated in the article). Credited to the integrated response team (통합대응단, including emergency blocking of illegal phone numbers), special crackdowns and strikes on overseas bases; no per-measure numbers. Single reporting number 1394 from 1 Feb 2026. Naver (with police) to detect scenario keywords in chats and warn users. — [Boannews, 26 Feb 2026](https://www.boannews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=142386)
- [PRESS citing local police] Daegu police prevention results rose from 24 cases / 790m won (Mar 2024 - Feb 2025) to 183 cases / 12.4bn won (Mar 2025 - Jan 2026) after a change in their prevention system (details not read). — search summary of [kbmaeil.com, 5 Feb 2026](https://kbmaeil.com/article/20260205500023)
- [VENDOR via PRESS] **KT AI voice-phishing detection** (network-side, real-time): in H1 2025 analysed about 14.6m calls, "detection accuracy of 91.6%", estimated prevented damage about 71bn won (KT estimate); v2.0 from 30 Jul 2025 adds speaker recognition using criminals' voiceprints from the National Forensic Service and deep-voice detection, target >95% accuracy and >200bn won prevented per year. — [Financial News (fnnews) English, 29 Jul 2025](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202507290951429524); [Mobile World Live](https://mobileworldlive.com/asia-pacific/kt-trials-voice-phishing-detection) (search summaries; "accuracy" not defined)
- [VENDOR via PRESS] **LG Uplus**: ixi-O (Nov 2024) does on-device real-time voice-phishing detection (initially iPhone 14+ only) and warns the user to hang up — [Korea JoongAng Daily, 2024](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/lg-us-ai-assistant-ixi-o-is-a-phishing-fighter-chat-tracker/12348462). LG Uplus tracked ~800 malicious-app control servers; ~33,000 customers had connected to them in 2025; it contacted 30,000+ at-risk customers, sometimes visiting homes with Seoul police to stop transfers in progress; its own estimate of prevented damage "about 1.8 trillion won" (= customers x police average loss, an upper-bound method) — [Digital Today (Japanese ed.)](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/jp/view/34870/lgu-tracks-malicious-app-servers-to-protect-30000-customers-from-voice-phishing); [LG press release](https://www.lg.co.kr/media/release/28799). A separate claim of 209bn won prevented "over the past three months" — [Mobile World Live](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/?p=435101). The two LG figures conflict; both are vendor estimates.
- [OFFICIAL, OECD incident database] KT's AI analysis of suspicious lines (number spoofing devices) led to seizure of ~5,000 devices and 54 arrests (June 2026 entry). — [OECD.AI incident, 18 Jun 2026](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2026-06-18-4caf)

### Inferences
- Korea is the warning case for India: payment-stop and 30-minute delayed-withdrawal rules (bank-side friction) did not stop the impersonation wave because scammers moved to cash handovers, crypto and victims taking out loans themselves; losses per case doubled. Friction that the victim can satisfy (waiting 30 minutes) does not work on a victim who believes they are cooperating with a prosecutor.
- What did reach self-confined victims was physical and human: motel staff, posters inside the room, patrol officers, bank tellers. The scam's weak point is the *isolation instruction* itself ("don't tell anyone", "stay alone", "buy a second phone", "use Telegram"), which is highly specific and detectable in text or by a family member.
- Telco AI detection is the most "moment of risk" of all, but needs carrier access; its published outcomes are vendor estimates using average-loss multipliers, which inflate "prevented" figures.

### Gaps
- No evaluation found of the 30-minute delayed-withdrawal rule (introduced 2012, threshold 1m won) or of the payment-stop (지급정지) rule's effect on losses.
- No full-year 2025 KT / SKT / LG Uplus figures with a stated method; no SKT outcome numbers found.
- National count of bank-teller voice-phishing preventions in 2025 not found.

## Japan: "fake police" special fraud (ニセ警察詐欺), staff interventions at banks / convenience stores, ATM limits, call blocking, anti-fraud phone devices

### Takeaway
Japan's 2025 "fake police" fraud is almost exactly India's digital arrest (fake arrest warrant, "prove your innocence by submitting assets", constant video calls "including while bathing or in the toilet") and it doubled special-fraud losses to a record ¥142.3bn. The 30-year-old human safety net (bank and convenience-store staff questioning customers) still stopped 17,962 cases worth ¥15.27bn in 2025, but its prevention rate fell 9.5 points to 39.8% because victims are now younger and pay by internet banking, which no teller sees. The NPA's own conclusion is that "once you are in contact with the criminals, anyone can be deceived", so it now prioritises blocking contact (international-call suspension, police-recommended blocking apps) over warning people mid-scam.

### Cited Findings
All from the NPA's 2025 confirmed statistics unless noted — [NPA, 特殊詐欺及びSNS型投資・ロマンス詐欺の認知・検挙状況等（令和7年・確定値）, PDF, 2026](https://www.npa.go.jp/bureau/criminal/souni/tokusyusagi/hurikomesagi_toukei2025.pdf); summary page [NPA SOS47, 5 Jun 2026](https://www.npa.go.jp/bureau/safetylife/sos47/new-topics/260605/01.html):
- [OFFICIAL] Special fraud 2025: 27,832 cases (+32.3%), about ¥142.31bn lost (+98.0%), "the highest ever". SNS investment fraud: 9,523 cases, ¥128.80bn; SNS romance fraud: 5,645 cases, ¥54.64bn. All fraud incl. SNS investment / romance: ¥325.7bn record. — NPA PDF above; [MIC press release, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_sosiki/joho_tsusin/eng/pressrelease/2026/7/29_1.html)
- [OFFICIAL] **Fake police fraud** (counted from 2025): "認知件数は11,014件、被害額は1,005.0億円で、総認知件数に占める割合は39.6％" (11,014 cases, ¥100.5bn, 39.6% of cases); "令和６年後半頃から被害の増加が顕著" (surging since late 2024) and "the main cause" of the 2025 increase. - [OFFICIAL] Payment channel shift: transfer-type cases 16,894 (60.7% of cases); **internet banking used in 6,866 transfer cases = 40.6% of transfer cases and 60.2% of transfer losses (¥49.53bn)**; crypto-transfer type 1,243 cases (+927%), ¥19.69bn; cash hand-over 14.6% of cases. Hyogo prefecture: in fake-police cases internet banking was 45.2% of cases and working-age people (20-59) 66.1% of victims. — NPA PDF; [Hyogo Prefectural Police, 2025 confirmed statistics, PDF](https://www.police.pref.hyogo.lg.jp/tokusyusagi/toukei/data/2025/R07kakutei.pdf)
- [OFFICIAL] **Staff interventions**: "コンビニエンスストア店員や金融機関職員等による声掛け等により、17,962件（-2,005件、-10.0％）、152.7億円（+60.8億円、+66.2％）の被害を防止（阻止率 39.8％（-9.5ポイント））" — convenience-store clerks, bank staff etc. prevented 17,962 cases / ¥15.27bn; prevention rate (prevented / (completed + prevented)) 39.8%, down 9.5 points. Historical: 2016: 13,140 cases prevented, rate 49.8% (search summary of [Zenkoku Shakyo PDF of NPA data, 2017](https://www2.shakyo.or.jp/old/pdf/topics/10_H28tokusyusagi_jyokyo.pdf)); 2019: ~30% of prevented cases were through financial-institution staff (press, same summary).

International numbers used in special fraud: 47,596 (2024) -> 92,996 (2025). ### Inferences
- The Japanese numbers are the cleanest demonstration of suspicion (b): staff warnings still save ¥15bn a year, but their share of attempts stopped is falling because the payment moved to the victim's own phone (internet banking, crypto), where no human sees it.
- They also show the limit of in-call warnings: the NPA explicitly argues that warnings after contact are weak against this scam and invests in contact blocking. For a one-student tool, the transferable pieces are (1) a "should I block international calls / unknown numbers?" setup helper and (2) catching the *reconnaissance* and *first-contact* stage, not only the payment stage.
- The NPA-recommended-app scheme is a government endorsement route open to third-party developers (apps that meet criteria get recommended) — the Indian analog would be getting a tool listed by I4C / cybercrime.gov.in or a state police cyber cell.

### Gaps
- No controlled evaluation of call-recording warning devices, ATM limits or the Osaka phone-at-ATM ban was found.
- National split of prevented cases by convenience store vs bank for 2025 not extracted.
