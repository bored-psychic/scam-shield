## 1. Totals: reported cyber-financial fraud losses and complaints, 2022-2026 (I4C / NCRP / CFCFRMS, NCRB, RBI)

### Takeaway
(draft) Reported losses on NCRP rose from about Rs 2,306 crore (2022) to Rs 7,465 crore (2023) to Rs 22,845.73 crore (2024), and stayed about flat at Rs 22,495 crore in 2025 (24+ lakh complaints). Cumulative 2021-2025: 65.89 lakh financial-fraud complaints, Rs 55,050 crore reported. Amount "saved" via CFCFRMS: Rs 11,158 crore in 32.80 lakh complaints, cumulative to 30 June 2026.
