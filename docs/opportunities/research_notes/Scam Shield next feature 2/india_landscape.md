## Google (Android, Pixel, Messages, Play Protect, Google Pay)

### Takeaway
(draft) Google's strongest India-wide layer is Play Protect's block on sideloaded apps that ask for SMS / notification / accessibility permissions (pilot since Oct 2024). The in-call protections are narrow: call Scam Detection is Pixel 9+ only (<1% of Indian phones); the screen-share alert is a pilot with 3 apps (Google Pay, Paytm, Navi).

## (raw, batch 7) RBI discussion paper (PRIMARY) — 2026-04-09
Source: [RBI, "Exploring safeguards in digital payments to curb frauds", 2026-04-09](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810); press release 2026-2027/57 [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=62535); comments closed 2026-05-08.
- Option 1 "lagged credit": 1-hour lag at payer's bank for APP transfers >= Rs 10,000 per transaction; individuals / sole props / partnerships; merchant payments, e-mandates/NACH, cheques exempt; payer can whitelist payees and cancel during the lag; mandatory vs optional left open (Q4).
- Option 2 trusted person: mandatory for 70+ and persons with disabilities, optional for others; APP transfers > Rs 50,000; 24 h cooling to change the trusted person or opt out.
- Option 3 low-credit-turnover accounts: Rs 25 lakh annual credit ceiling by default; excess is "shadow credit" usable after verification, returned after 30 days.
- Option 4 customer controls: per-channel on/off + limits; one-action kill switch; reactivation via authenticated channel or branch; open question whether new accounts are "default off".
- Already in place / in development per RBI: tokenisation, card limits, 2025 authentication framework, MNRL check on DoT DIP, 1600xx/1400xx call series, MuleHunter.AI, proposed Digital Payment Intelligence Platform (real-time beneficiary risk score).
- NCRP fraud series (reported frauds / value): 2021 2.6 lakh / Rs 551 cr; 2022 6.9 lakh / 2,290 cr; 2023 13.1 lakh / 7,465 cr; 2024 24 lakh / 22,848 cr; 2025 28 lakh / 22,931 cr.
- Transactions > Rs 10,000 = ~45% of reported fraud cases by volume and ~98.5% by value; ~92% of value is in frauds > Rs 50,000 (NCRP). => money at stake is concentrated in a few large push payments.
- Paper on manipulation: "Victims, acting under deception, themselves initiate and authenticate transactions"; fraudsters rely on "creating urgency and maintaining continuous psychological pressure"; post-transaction recovery has "low recovery rates".
- Precedents cited: UK 72-h hold (PSR 2024), Singapore 12-h cooling-off + kill switch, Sweden, Ireland trusted contact, US trusted contact, Australia "digital padlock".
- Status as of 2026-10-22: proposal only; no final direction found (to re-check).
- PhonePe Protect press release dated 2025-11-03: declines Very High FRI numbers with alert, warns on Medium; describes mobile-number payments; no performance numbers — [PhonePe](https://www.phonepe.com/press/phonepe-rolls-out-phonepe-protect-to-safeguard-users-from-fraudulent-transactions/)
