A rule or tactic that fires on the folded text and did not on the raw text is `r["fold_hits"]`; it becomes one evidence `fold.hidden` (tag "Disguised text", caution on a warning, neutral otherwise) and, on a warning verdict, one reason line "Hidden or look-alike characters were used to disguise the wording."
