- Retina flicker fix (2026-10-21; frontend only): on a 1710x1073 @2x window the black hole's light page vanished for ~1.3 s but for a tile, and the comet showed dark rectangles. **Cause (traced):** Chrome draws each clip-path as a "Synthesized Clip" mask layer, sized by the clip path's own bounds and re-rasterized as tiles every frame; 10 full-screen masks (root, vignette x2, main x2, and the header's and footer's even-odd "outside" clips spanned the whole screen) peaked at 611 MB of active tiles against an 801 MB limit (pending + active trees), 24/54 tile assignments ran out of memory and whole 1710x536 px tiles went undrawn. `--force-gpu-mem-available-mb=8192` removed it (confirmation only; no flag in the fix). **Fix:** (1) `clipInside` / `clipOutside` take a box and cut the polygon to it (`cutTo`): each layer's reach = its rect + 48 px (`INK`), a pane's rect + 2 px; (2) the vignette's two snapshots are hidden (`.ss-vigpaint`) and both looks are painted on a canvas `.ss-vigc` each frame (`vignetteLook` rebuilds the computed radial gradient; anything else falls back to clipped snapshots). Either alone was not enough (2/120 and 9/120 failing frames at 1710 @2x); together 0/120, 414 MB peak, 3 full-screen masks, no OOM; 1920x1200 and 2560x1440 @2x pass too. **Checks** (`e2e/switch.mjs`, headed Chrome, `--frames-only` for just these): S14 every 5 % (0-95 %) of both animations at 1710x1073 @2, 1440x900 @2, 1280x800 @1, 390x844 @3: 8 px samples classed by the nearer --bg token, counted only where both still pages agree, must be on the polygon's side (fail: > 2 % wrong or a 150-sample patch; noise <= 1.1 %, failures 24-68 %); S15 the vignette canvas vs the page's own, each theme on its side: <= 4/255; S16 headed frame times at 1710x1073 @2x, reported only: the switch runs ~26-41 fps there (before ~23-28; idle 16.6 ms; 60 fps at 1280x800 @2x headless): **not 60 fps on a 1710-wide Retina screen, open** (see Retina frame time below).
