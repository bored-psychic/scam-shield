- Theme-switch animation (2026-10-18; frontend only; replaces the 0.3 s cross-fade): `src/lib/themeSwitch.ts` ports the approved prototype `docs/design/theme-switch.html` (its comet(), hole(), shield(), toScreen(); the Tuner is not built): comet dark -> light 2.4 s with a 34 degree cone, black hole light -> dark 2.6 s. **How:** `document.startViewTransition`; the theme attribute changes in its callback; every frame a *paused* Web Animation on `::view-transition-new(root)` (comet) or `::view-transition-old(root)` (black hole, put on top) gets new `clip-path: polygon()` keyframes, cut to the screen first (Chrome mis-draws far off-screen points). The paused animation keeps the transition alive until the script calls `finish()`, so time comes only from `performance.now()` and Playwright's clock can stop it anywhere. Effects (comet, shock, hole, streaks, shield at 1.1 x the globe's radius) on one canvas `.ss-fx` with `view-transition-name: ss-fx`, created in the callback (no old view) and removed at the end; the switch has `ss-btn` and sits on top in its new state from the first frame (solid `--bg` fill during the black hole: its glass has no backdrop in its own snapshot). The globe **holds still** during the switch (`holdGlobe` in `Globe.tsx`, as the prototype's globe does) and is repainted synchronously in the callback (`repaintGlobe`), so both snapshots show it at the same angle and it changes along exactly the page's edge; `globeCircle()` gives its screen circle from the camera. Colours: `--fx-*` "r, g, b" tokens. State, label and stored choice change on the click; clicks during the switch are ignored; a resize or a hidden tab skips the transition (already in the new theme). Reduced motion, no View Transitions or a hidden tab: instant switch (no simpler fallback). CSS under `.ss-switch` / `.ss-comet` / `.ss-hole` (end of `styles.css`); transitions are off during the switch so the new page is snapshotted in its final colours.
