- Theme-switch animation (2026-10-18; frontend only; replaces the 0.3 s cross-fade): `src/lib/themeSwitch.ts` ports the approved prototype `docs/design/theme-switch.html` (its comet(), hole(), shield(), toScreen(); the Tuner is not built): comet dark -> light 2.4 s with a 34 degree cone, black hole light -> dark 2.6 s. **How:** `document.startViewTransition`; the theme attribute changes in its callback; every frame one clip polygon (the light side) cuts the snapshots, as *paused* Web Animations on the pseudo-elements whose `clip-path` keyframes are replaced each frame (cut to the screen first: Chrome mis-draws far off-screen points). The paused animations keep the transition alive until the script calls `finish()`, so time comes only from `performance.now()` and Playwright's clock can stop it anywhere. **The globe keeps turning (2026-10-19):** the page is split into snapshot groups, bottom to top: root (only the page background; the comet clips its new view, the black hole puts its old one on top), `ss-globe` (the stage, live new view only), `ss-vig`, `.ss-pane` (one empty pane per glass panel within 1.6 globe radii: its group wears the panel's backdrop-filter and shape on the dark side, so the dark glass blurs the live globe; a panel's own snapshot has nothing behind it to blur), `ss-main` / `ss-head` / `ss-foot` / `ss-tip` (light view clipped to the polygon, dark view to its even-odd complement: `clipInside` / `clipOutside`, offset to the layer's box), `ss-fx`, `ss-btn`. Not `body` as one group: Chrome draws body's fixed children (footer, vignette) about 35 px low in its old view. While a pane wears a panel's glass the panel drops its own (`.ss-glassed`, keeping `will-change: backdrop-filter`): in its snapshot it would filter a neighbour's shadow and draw it twice. The switch draws the globe's frames itself (`clipGlobe` in `Globe.tsx`): two passes through a stencil cut from the same polygon (a triangle fan inverting bit 0; MSAA per sample), the old look on one side and the new one on the other, a single pass while the globe's reach is all on one side (`boxSide`); the globe's own loop waits and resumes counting the time since the switch's last frame; all shaders are compiled at mount (`warm()`). No arc starts during a switch; arcs in flight go 1.5 x and land as usual (one ripple, one footer hit); arcs start again when it ends, also after a skipped one. - Retina flicker fix (2026-10-21; frontend only): on a 1710x1073 @2x window the black hole's light page vanished for ~1.3 s but for a tile, and the comet showed dark rectangles. **Cause (traced):** Chrome draws each clip-path as a "Synthesized Clip" mask layer, sized by the clip path's own bounds and re-rasterized as tiles every frame; 10 full-screen masks (root, vignette x2, main x2, and the header's and footer's even-odd "outside" clips spanned the whole screen) peaked at 611 MB of active tiles against an 801 MB limit (pending + active trees), 24/54 tile assignments ran out of memory and whole 1710x536 px tiles went undrawn. `--force-gpu-mem-available-mb=8192` removed it (confirmation only; no flag in the fix). **Fix:** (1) `clipInside` / `clipOutside` take a box and cut the polygon to it (`cutTo`): each layer's reach = its rect + 48 px (`INK`), a pane's rect + 2 px; (2) the vignette's two snapshots are hidden (`.ss-vigpaint`) and both looks are painted on a canvas `.ss-vigc` each frame (`vignetteLook` rebuilds the computed radial gradient; anything else falls back to clipped snapshots). Either alone was not enough (2/120 and 9/120 failing frames at 1710 @2x); together 0/120, 414 MB peak, 3 full-screen masks, no OOM; 1920x1200 and 2560x1440 @2x pass too. **Checks** (`e2e/switch.mjs`, headed Chrome, `--frames-only` for just these): S14 every 5 % (0-95 %) of both animations at 1710x1073 @2, 1440x900 @2, 1280x800 @1, 390x844 @3: 8 px samples classed by the nearer --bg token, counted only where both still pages agree, must be on the polygon's side (fail: > 2 % wrong or a 150-sample patch; noise <= 1.1 %, failures 24-68 %); S15 the vignette canvas vs the page's own, each theme on its side: <= 4/255; S16 headed frame times at 1710x1073 @2x, reported only: the switch runs ~26-41 fps there (before ~23-28; idle 16.6 ms; 60 fps at 1280x800 @2x headless): **not 60 fps on a 1710-wide Retina screen, open** (see Retina frame time below).
