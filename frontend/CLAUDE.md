- Theme-switch animation (2026-10-18; frontend only; replaces the 0.3 s cross-fade): `src/lib/themeSwitch.ts` ports the approved prototype `docs/design/theme-switch.html` (its comet(), hole(), shield(), toScreen(); the Tuner is not built): comet dark -> light 2.4 s with a 34 degree cone, black hole light -> dark 2.6 s. **How:** `document.startViewTransition`; the theme attribute changes in its callback; every frame one clip polygon (the light side) cuts the snapshots, as *paused* Web Animations on the pseudo-elements whose `clip-path` keyframes are replaced each frame (cut to the screen first: Chrome mis-draws far off-screen points). The paused animations keep the transition alive until the script calls `finish()`, so time comes only from `performance.now()` and Playwright's clock can stop it anywhere. **The globe keeps turning (2026-10-19):** the page is split into snapshot groups, bottom to top: root (only the page background; the comet clips its new view, the black hole puts its old one on top), `ss-globe` (the stage, live new view only), `ss-vig`, `.ss-pane` (one empty pane per glass panel within 1.6 globe radii: its group wears the panel's backdrop-filter and shape on the dark side, so the dark glass blurs the live globe; a panel's own snapshot has nothing behind it to blur), `ss-main` / `ss-head` / `ss-foot` / `ss-tip` (light view clipped to the polygon, dark view to its even-odd complement: `clipInside` / `clipOutside`, offset to the layer's box), `ss-fx`, `ss-btn`. Not `body` as one group: Chrome draws body's fixed children (footer, vignette) about 35 px low in its old view. While a pane wears a panel's glass the panel drops its own (`.ss-glassed`, keeping `will-change: backdrop-filter`): in its snapshot it would filter a neighbour's shadow and draw it twice. The switch draws the globe's frames itself (`clipGlobe` in `Globe.tsx`): two passes through a stencil cut from the same polygon (a triangle fan inverting bit 0; MSAA per sample), the old look on one side and the new one on the other, a single pass while the globe's reach is all on one side (`boxSide`); the globe's own loop waits and resumes counting the time since the switch's last frame; all shaders are compiled at mount (`warm()`). No arc starts during a switch; arcs in flight go 1.5 x and land as usual (one ripple, one footer hit); arcs start again when it ends, also after a skipped one.
