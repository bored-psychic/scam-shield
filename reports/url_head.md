# URL head

Generated by `src/url_head.py`. HistGradientBoosting (200 iters, lr 0.1, 31 leaves, l2 1.0, `class_weight=balanced`, no tuning), fit on the train split of the phishing-vs-benign population only. Thresholds are chosen on val (max F1) and applied unchanged to test and to the held-out feeds. Domain-grouped splits from `urls.csv`.

## Domain age coverage (phishing-vs-benign rows)
