## 1. The two patterns

Masking is applied **after** `model_text`, AMT first, then DATE: `AMT -> " <AMT> "`, `DATE -> " <DATE> "`. Regexes (case-insensitive):

```
AMT  = (?<![A-Za-z])(?:Rs\.?|INR|₹)\s*\d+(?:,\d+)*(?:\.\d+)?
DATE = (?<!\d)(?:0?[1-9]|[12]\d|3[01])(?P<sep>[-/.])(?:0?[1-9]|1[0-2])(?P=sep)(?:\d{4}|\d{2})(?!\d)|(?<!\d)(?:0?[1-9]|[12]\d|3[01])(?:st|nd|rd|th)?[ \-/]?(?:jan(?:uary)?|feb(?:ruary)?|mar(?:ch)?|apr(?:il)?|(?-i:May|MAY)|june?|july?|aug(?:ust)?|sep(?:t(?:ember)?)?|oct(?:ober)?|nov(?:ember)?|dec(?:ember)?)\b(?:[ \-/,]*(?:\d{4}|\d{2})(?!\d))?|\b(?:jan(?:uary)?|feb(?:ruary)?|mar(?:ch)?|apr(?:il)?|(?-i:May|MAY)|june?|july?|aug(?:ust)?|sep(?:t(?:ember)?)?|oct(?:ober)?|nov(?:ember)?|dec(?:ember)?)\.?[ \-/]?(?:0?[1-9]|[12]\d|3[01])(?:st|nd|rd|th)?(?!\d)(?:,?[ \-/]*(?:\d{4}|\d{2})(?!\d))?
```

- AMT: `Rs`, `Rs.`, `INR` or the rupee sign, optional spaces, then a number with comma groups and decimals (`Rs. 2,500.00`, `INR9.50`, `Rs500`). `hrs 5` / `Mrs 5` are excluded. **Not covered:** `rupees 500`, `$500`, `5,000/-`, `500 rs`.
- DATE: `dd-mm-yy(yy)`, `dd/mm/yy(yy)`, `dd.mm.yy(yy)` (same separator twice, day 1-31, month 1-12), plus `dd Mon` and `Mon dd` with a month name, optional separator, optional `st/nd/rd/th`, optional year (`02-Mar-26`, `5 March`, `March 5, 2026`). Capital-M `May` only. **Not covered:** `yyyy-mm-dd`, `mm/yy`, `March 2026`, times.
- Both are regexes on the text the model reads (after links became `<URL>` and the cut to 2,000 characters), so a row counts only if the pattern is inside what the model sees.
- Rows whose model input changes under the mask: train 5,553 of 37,812, val 752 of 5,267.

## Interpretation
<!-- numbers: 85c5b099af63 -->
Written by hand on 2026-10-17 from the tables above. **Verdict: the baseline does not lean on amount and date formatting enough to matter; what effect there is, is small, local and goes the wrong way for masking. Keep as is.**

1. **The corpus does contain the associations, with mixed signs (section 2).** Amount => scam: `scam_ham_india` 99.3% scam with an amount vs 30.1% without (288 train rows; val 97.0% vs 23.2%), `ealvaradob_phishing` 100% (38 rows), but flat in `india_fraud_final` (44.5% vs 53.5%, 751 rows). Date => benign: `india_fraud_final` 0 of 386 dated train rows are scam (val 0 of 18), `synth_india` 0 of 136, `phishing_email` 10.8% vs 46.0% (3,554 rows); date => scam in the two small sources `scam_ham_india` (84.0%, 81 rows) and `uci_sms_spam` (81.4%, 43 rows). So a model *could* learn "date means safe" from the India-like source.
2. **The trained baseline barely uses them (section 3).** Masking both patterns moves val AUROC by -0.0002 (95% interval -0.0007 to +0.0003); F1 at the val-chosen cut-offs moves by +0.002 (likely), -0.002 (flagged), -0.005 (at 0.5), scam recall at the suspicious cut-off 0.952 -> 0.951. Per-source deltas run from -0.001 to +0.002, and the only interval that does not straddle zero (`uci_sms_spam`, +0.000 to +0.005) belongs to a source with 2 pattern rows, so it shows the size of refit noise, not of a mask effect. `fake_job_postings` is the weakest source (AUROC 0.805, 99 dated rows) and does not move (-0.001).
3. **Level flips are close to the noise floor (section 4).** Rows without a pattern have identical input; at the same cut-offs refit drift alone changes the level of 24 of 4,515 of them (0.5%), including 7 benign rows newly flagged and 1 scam row newly missed. Pattern rows change 17 of 752 (2.3%): 4x the rate, but in absolute terms +8 / -5 benign rows flagged / cleared and 1 scam row missed. The cut-off shift (likely 0.656 -> 0.641) is not the cause: the pattern-row counts are identical at (i)'s cut-offs. By pattern: amount-only masking flips 8 benign rows to flagged and 1 to cleared, all in `india_fraud_final`, **but those 8 rows are 2 clusters**, i.e. two templates, which is thin evidence; date-only masking clears 4 benign `phishing_email` rows and misses 1 scam row. No scam row is newly caught in any pattern row.
4. **Genuine alerts (section 5).** None changes level; all three stay "No warning signs" under both models (cut-off 0.423). Scores move -0.039 (HDFC alert, 0.147 -> 0.108), +0.043 (`otp-disclose`, 0.335 -> 0.378) and -0.020 (dated HDFC alert, 0.164 -> 0.144): mixed sign. The largest move, `otp-disclose`, goes *toward* the cut-off (0.378 against 0.425, about half the remaining distance) without crossing it, so masking is not uniformly safer for genuine alerts. The BUGS.md observation is reproduced (` rs ` is +0.200 / +0.171 / +0.118 toward scam, `-26` is -0.093 toward safe), but masking does not remove the cue: the new `<AMT>` token picks up +0.131 / +0.101 / +0.082 (`'t> '`), because amount presence is itself a scam cue in training (`scam_ham_india`, `ealvaradob_phishing`). The cue is "an amount is present", not how it is spelled; masking would only rename it. (The message in BUGS.md is not in the repo, so these numbers differ from its 0.229; the closest messages were used.)
5. **Recommendation: keep as is.** Do not add `<AMT>` / `<DATE>` masking to `textprep.py` at `splits-v2` (CLAUDE.md rule 11 stays unchanged): it buys no AUROC, its one visible effect is 2 benign `india_fraud_final` templates losing their "safe" verdict, it does not remove the amount cue, and a rule-11 change would force the baseline, the fine-tuning script and every saved model to be retrained or rescored.

**Not measured, and why it matters.** (a) The attack direction: this ablation removes the cue from the input but never *adds* it. Where dated rows are 0 of 386 scam (`india_fraud_final`), a scammer who appends a date or an amount could push a message toward safe. That belongs in the held-out attack suite (insert `on 14-10-26` / `Rs 500` into scam parents of the same split), not here. (b) MuRIL reads digits differently; repeat the ablation on its val predictions only if its per-source results suggest it. (c) Hindi and Kannada have 0 val rows. (d) Amount and date forms outside the regexes (`$`, `rupees`, `5,000/-`, `yyyy-mm-dd`, `26Feb26`). (e) Only about 43 benign val rows contain an amount (116 AMT rows, 62.9% scam), so "genuine bank alerts" are thinly covered in val. (f) Side finding, not an amount or date effect: the largest push on the dated alert is `xxx` (+0.190 -> +0.226), the `1800-XXX-XXXX` placeholder of the design-prototype string; real alerts do not carry it.
