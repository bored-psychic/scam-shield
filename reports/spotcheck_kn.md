## The one note: kn22

- **kn22** (`p00696`, scam, `india_fraud_final`, frozen train row): English "Dear, you have passed our interview. Salary is 23,00/month. Please contact to discuss detail." Kannada "... ಸಂಬಳವು 23,00 ತಿಂಗಳು ..." = "salary 23,00 months". The "/month" was turned into a bare noun and the "per" is lost. Reviewer note: "per month should be mentioned in this text which is translated". The scam ask is unaffected, so Q2 stays y.
- **Siblings (my check, not the reviewer's).** All 37 kept Kannada rows whose English parent matches a rate or time pattern were read: 25 are only a time of day ("9:30 PM"), 2 are a "daily" data quota (kept as ದೈನಂದಿನ), and 10 state a pay rate. Of those 10, 7 keep the "per" (ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ, ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ, ವಾರಕ್ಕೆ, ತಿಂಗಳಿಗೆ, ಮಾಸಿಕ) and 3 lose it: kn22 and two more that have the same slash-rate form ("X/month", "X/day") and the same omission: `p00720` ("Rs 30,000/month" -> "Rs 30,000 ತಿಂಗಳನ್ನು ಗಳಿಸಿ", the month as an object) and `p00719` ("8000-20000/day" -> "8000-20000 ದಿನವನ್ನು ಗಳಿಸಲು", the day as an object). Neither was on the sheet. All three are frozen train rows, scam, so none touches val or test.
- **Dropped from the Kannada augmentation set (at the rebuild, not applied yet):** kn22 / `p00696`, `p00720`, `p00719`. 587 -> 584 rows (scam 88 -> 85, benign 499 unchanged, scam share 15.0% -> 14.6%). List with hashes: `docs/spotcheck/exclude_kn_v1.txt`. No translated text was edited by hand.

## Limits

- **One reviewer, 47 rows.** No second opinion and no agreement measure. The 47 are 8.0% of the 587 kept Kannada rows; the other 540 are unchecked. The Wilson lower bound (92.4%) clears the gate on the sheet, but the sheet is not the pool (above) and 24 scam rows cannot rule out a few percent of bad scam rows.
- **47 of 47 identical marks.** Hindi from the same sheet design had 4 failing rows and 18 `formal` flags; Kannada has none of either. That fits both a cleaner translation and a more lenient or less sensitive review, and these data cannot tell the two apart. Spot-checks of my own, outside the sheet, found defects in rows the reviewer never saw, for example `p00174` (benign, no split yet): the brand "Licious" came out as "ವಿಷಪೂರಿತ" ("poisonous"), and `p00720`'s "URGENT HIRING" became "ತುರ್ತು ಬಾಡಿಗೆ" ("urgent rent"). Both rows are in the unchecked 540 (p00720 is dropped above for its rate; the rent mistake is a separate defect I noticed in passing). Treat 100% as an upper estimate for the pool.
- **`formal` was never flagged**, so there is no Kannada register figure to compare with Hindi's (38% on the sheet, about 27% re-weighted). It is a bare flag; do not read 0% as "reads like real Kannada SMS".
- **Same shortcut flag as before:** the benign translations are bank / OTP alerts, the scam ones prose (digit/mask cue AUROC 0.80 for the translations alone). Any Kannada metric is labelled 'translated'.
- **Split membership of the 100 sheet rows** is recorded in `reports/spotcheck_hi.md` (identical counts for both sheets): 50 benign rows with no split yet, 50 scam rows 44 train / 4 dev / 2 test; the Kannada one is `kn18` (`p00691`, answered y / y) and was not edited.
- **Assumption on ids.** The export carries ids only; I assume the reviewer saw the committed sheet. kn22's note matches the text at `kn22` in that sheet, which corroborates it.
- **Re-running `translate_parents.py finalise` rewrites `reports/spotcheck_kn.csv`.** The sha256 of the rows file is in the block above; if it changes, the scores no longer describe the sheet the reviewer saw.

## Decision

D26 in `docs/decisions/README.md`: Kannada translations are accepted for the same use as Hindi in D20, **training augmentation only**, never counted as real data, never added to val or test, any Kannada metric labelled 'translated'. Gate met on the point estimates (47/47 on both questions, 100% pool-weighted, 97.9% / 99.4% if kn22 is counted as an n). Conditions: the three rows in `exclude_kn_v1.txt` are left out of training at the rebuild, and the D21 digit / URL check applies to Kannada as it does to Hindi. This unblocks the translation rebuild and `splits-v2`; it does not run them.
