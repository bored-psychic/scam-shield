## Reading the hits

The Tranco top 10k scan had one hit on the first run, `amazon[.]dev` (rank 133), Amazon's own developer site: added to Amazon's official list, as were `amazon.eu`, `amazon.jp`, `amazon.science`, `bankofbaroda.co.in` and `paytm.me` (found by the 100k scan; marked "added after the scan" in `exact_brands.json`, recognised by the author, ownership not re-checked). The hits that remain in the top 100k are other TLDs of Roblox, Zomato, Blinkit and BharatPe, and `amazon.work`. Whether they are the brand's own defensive registrations or someone else's cannot be told from the name, and checking needs a registry look-up (a network call), so they are left flagged: the effect is a Suspicious flag with the sentence "Uses the name X but is not X's official website" on a link to such a domain, and the fix for a confirmed one is a line in the brand's official list.
