What each step does:

- **marker restore failed (after the greedy retry)**: the model dropped or duplicated a number marker; strict restore, nothing is repaired by guessing
- **person name in the English parent (rule 5)**: NAME_RX, curated by eye, not exhaustive
- **digit or mask-token mismatch**: ASCII digits and <PHONE>/<ACCOUNT>/... tokens must equal the parent's, as multisets
- **wrong script**: a letter of another non-ASCII script, or no target-script letter at all
- **leftover English**: fewer than 50% of the letters outside the protected spans are in the target script
- **exact duplicate within the translations**: first kept
- **trim scam to ~15% of the translated rows**: target 92 scam rows for 523 benign (15% of the translated rows); 108 available; the 16 val/test scam rows are never trimmed (val/test scam is thin), the cut comes from train rows, most numeric kept (floor 3 per fraud mechanism)

### The person-name step (not in the original plan)

CLAUDE.md rule 5 says victim names are masked before text reaches `data/final/`, but `pii.MASKS` has no name pattern, and the benign parents (bank alerts, train tickets, delivery updates) carry real-looking names: UPI payers, a ticket passenger, a delivery rider, a doctor. 26 parents (26 benign, 0 scam) matched a short list of names found by reading the rare capitalised tokens of the parents file in context; their translations are dropped (the ledger shows how many were still alive at this step in each language; the rest had already failed the marker check). Matched tokens: ARUN x4, BHAKSHI x1, CHAKRAV x1, CHINNAYYA x1, GANDHARV x7, GOKULA x1, GOWRISHA x1, MAHBUBAN x1, MAHESH x1, PURUSHOTHAMA x1, RACHURI x1, RAJASHEKAR x1, RAVINDER x2, RENU x1, SALEEM x2, SIDDHARTH x1, SRINIVAS x1, VUTNOORI x2. **The list is curated, not exhaustive**: names the reading missed are still in the kept rows. The real fix is name masking in `pii.py` (BUGS.md). To undo this step, empty `NAME_RX`.

### Decision

Rule (instructions of 2026-10-07): decide against the English reference, not against 0.5. If the combined pool's cue AUROCs are at or below the English reference, accept and report them; if clearly above (interval entirely above), sample the translated rows by length bin and re-measure.

- **hindi**: combined-pool length-only AUROC 0.67 [0.58, 0.74] (synth_india alone: 0.63; the English reference: 0.92 [0.91, 0.92]). Models whose combined interval lies entirely above the English reference's interval: none. Gate verdict: **accept**. Length-bin sampling: **not applied**.
- **kannada**: combined-pool length-only AUROC 0.72 [0.63, 0.80] (synth_india alone: 0.53; the English reference: 0.92 [0.91, 0.92]). Models whose combined interval lies entirely above the English reference's interval: none. Gate verdict: **accept**. Length-bin sampling: **not applied**.

One earlier instruction used a literal trigger instead (length-only AUROC clearly above the synth baseline, say > 0.65); that trigger fires here. The reference rule is the later and more specific instruction and is the one followed. The length-bin sampling is therefore **off** (it is a flag, `--length-match`, not a default). Pass B below is why it should stay off.

## Caveats

- **Automatic filters do not check meaning.** Only the native-speaker spot-check does; build_text.py waits for it.
- **Benign diversity is low.** The kept benign translations (per language, about 523) come from 326 template groups of one preview dataset (provenance unknown); card-spend alerts and OTP messages dominate. They teach 'transactional alert in Hindi/Kannada = benign', which is the point, but not much beyond it.
- **Scam translations repeat English scam content already in `messages.csv`** (they are translations of those rows) and inherit their splits; val has 12 and test 5 scam parents in total, so val/test scam counts in Hindi/Kannada stay small.
- **Names**: see the person-name step above; the list is curated, and the existing `messages.csv` rows were not checked for names.
- **Leftover Latin is normal**: bank abbreviations (Txn, Avl Bal, A/c), brand and sender names stay Latin, as in the existing `synth_india` rows; only rows where most letters are Latin are dropped.
