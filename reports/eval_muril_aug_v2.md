### Risk meter (D24): how the 0-100 scale would map onto these scores (proposal, not applied)

Today `risk.py` maps the score linearly through (0 -> 0, suspicious cut-off -> 30, likely cut-off -> 70, 1 -> 100). With muril's cut-offs (0.005 / 0.030) the bands keep their meaning (the cut-offs still land on 30 / 70), but inside each band the number is driven by a few hundredths of probability. The proposal keeps the same anchors and interpolates on the **logit** scale, with the ends at the 1st / 99th percentile of the model's **val** logits (-7.98 -> 0, 8.58 -> 100; p = 0.00034 / 0.99981). Everything here is fit and shown on **val**; levels are unchanged by construction (only the number inside a band moves).
