## Open

- **D11 folded view: what it does not cover (2026-10-28, `src/shield/core/fold.py`, `reports/fold_v1.md`).** (1) Separators between letters (`O.T.P`, `O T P`, `o-t-p`) are not folded: that is the held-out `spaced_keywords` attack. (2) Leet is recovered only where the digit or symbol sits in a token with two letters or more and is not part of a number: a word-initial `@` only if "a" + the rest is a word in the rules' own patterns (`@ccount`, not `@mazon`), `R5 5000` / `R$ 5000` read as `Rs`, a run of three leet digits only between letters, `1` is read as `i` or `l` by the rules' word list and as `i` when neither is a known word. (3) The fold also changes harmless text in a few places (`Secret$` -> `Secrets` loses the bare `secret` cue; `TataTea1kg` -> `TataTeaikg`), with no rule consequence found on train or val. (4) A disguise inside a link is not undone (links are not touched), and the URL head and the link checks read the raw link. (5) Hindi / Kannada: only invisible characters are removed; Devanagari-for-Latin look-alikes are not folded; the Hindi / Kannada versions of the "hidden or look-alike characters" line (`fold.FOLD_LINE`) are unreviewed drafts and not in the Report, so the UI shows English only (D17). (6) `fold.hidden` also shows as a neutral note on a message with no warning when only a tactic cue needed the fold.

All four written gate conditions are met (0 val scams missed, 4 of 5 advisories lowered, 30/30 tricky, A1-A15) but the cap fired on 0 of 5,267 val rows, so the val check is empty, and pasting one warning sentence onto the 1,097 flagged val scams clears 38 (3.5%) or 32 (2.9%) of them (phishing_email, fake_job_postings; 5-9 of 430 SMS-sized scams too), so it was left OFF. **Open:** (1) enable or not (user's call; recommended: not until the retrained head is measured); the real fix is data and a head that reads word order: the benign OTP / bank-alert translations arrived with `splits-v2` (1,000 train rows, Hindi + Kannada; 2026-10-21), then retrain and rerun `src/otp_advisory.py`. - **Real-message intake (2026-10-25, `src/intake_real.py`): known limits.** (1) **A template group gets one label.** Groups are single-link near-duplicate clusters (digits collapsed), so a group can chain related templates: on the first SMS export (6,560 messages, 1,545 groups) the two biggest groups have an example similarity of 0.35 between their most common and their most different text, and 127 groups of size >= 4 have 6 below 0.5. Look at `example_1` / `example_2` / `example_similarity` and label such a group `skip` when the two examples would not get the same label. (2) **Language for SMS and WhatsApp is guessed from the script only**, so Hinglish is `en`; per-language real-message numbers undercount Hinglish. (3) **WhatsApp own messages are included**: an export does not say which lines are the owner's, so personal chit-chat becomes groups of size 1 (label them `skip`). (4) **Names are not masked automatically except** contact / sender names seen in the input, greeting names seen in >= 3 messages and `data/real/mask_names.txt`; everything else (an ALL-CAPS UPI payer, a name after "Dear") is a review candidate in `review_names.csv` and only `--freeze` insists it was looked at. A name used without a greeting by a stranger and rarer than 5 texts is offered; a recurring stranger's name without a greeting is not. (5) `eval_real.py` does not report per group yet (`group_id` / `group_size` are in the file). (6) The DLT suffix of a sender id (-S service, -P promotional, -T transactional, -G government) is dropped with the address; `sender_kind` only says it was an alphanumeric header.
