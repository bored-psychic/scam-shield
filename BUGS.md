## Open

- **OTP / PIN / CVV rule: known limits.** English plus a Hinglish `OTP bata do` form only (no Devanagari or Kannada patterns, on purpose: an unchecked pattern should not force a verdict). A genuine delivery-OTP instruction ("share the OTP with the delivery partner") fires it. `text`, `message`, `confirm` and `enter` are left out of the verb list (real OTP SMS use them), so "confirm your OTP" is not caught. A negation more than 3 words from the verb, or split by anything other than one comma-delimited aside, is not recognised and the sentence counts as a request. (The comma-interrupted negation, "Do not, under any circumstances, share your OTP", was fixed on 2026-10-09 and is in `tests/tricky_inputs.py`.)
- **Brand / keyword check on free-hosting subdomains escalates harmless names.** `pineapple-recipes.netlify.app` (brand `apple`: brands of 5+ letters match as a substring), `amazon-fan-blog.github.io` and `my-support-group.vercel.app` all become Suspicious; `freelance-dev.pages.dev` and `riverbank-photos.pages.dev` correctly stay "Can't judge" (keywords of under 5 letters must be whole words). This is the specified behaviour (any brand or scam keyword escalates), so a personal site with such a name gets a false Suspicious. The lists are hand-written and not exhaustive (24 shorteners, 36 free-hosting domains): `kozow.com`, `ooguy.com` and `giize.com` appear in the OpenPhish misses of `reports/url_head.md` and look like free subdomain services, but that was not checked, so they are not listed.
- **Host-only URL head is weak, and its brand cue is nearly dead.** Test AUROC 0.859; at the val threshold 8.3% of Tranco and 9.1% of ealvaradob benign test URLs are flagged (`reports/url_head.md`). `https://www.google.com/` scores 0.484 (flag 0.537), `https://www.hdfcbank.com/` 0.263. Permutation importance: `tld_code` 0.128, `brand_token_offsite` 0.002; on `http://sbi-kyc-verify-now.xyz/login` the brand feature's occlusion effect is -0.016 (toward benign). All 15,726 train Tranco benign rows are bare registered domains, while 35.6% of train ealvaradob benign and 47.4% of its phishing rows have a subdomain, so a leading `www.` may read as a phishing cue (not tested). The full-URL view is better (test AUROC 0.939, Tranco FPR 4.1%), but `models/url_head.joblib` is the host-only one. Fix: strip `www.` before scoring, add benign hosts with subdomains, ablate the brand features, and decide which view the app should load.
- **The text head leans on amount and date formatting.** On a genuine HDFC debit alert (score 0.229, level 0) the strongest n-gram is ` rs ` (+0.251 toward scam) and date pieces (`26.`, `-26`, `02`) push toward safe. One message, not measured across the corpus. Check: the scam rate of rows containing `Rs` per source, and a run with amounts and dates masked (`<AMT>`, `<DATE>`).
- **`<URL>` presence is still a scam cue in `synth_india`.** In every language 58% of its scam rows contain a link against 14% of its benign rows. After `<URL>` replaced the domains, one benign Kannada family (`synth_courier_customs_0_3_1`, 15 test rows, all with a link) went from correct to false positive (Kannada accuracy 0.824 -> 0.751, scam recall unchanged; Hindi did not move). Fix: give the benign templates links at the scam rate (a splits-v2 change), or ablate `<URL>` for this source. `reports/url_token.md`.
- **`reports/sanity.md` Interpretation predates the `<URL>` input.** The tables were regenerated on 2026-10-09; the hand-written section still quotes 0.895 and the old LOSO values (0.676 / 0.707 ...) and carries a generated **Stale** warning. Rewrite it (CLAUDE.md: Opus, high) and mention `model_text` in it, which removes the warning.
- **Tactic tags are untuned keyword lists.** `authority` fires on any `bank`, `sbi`, `hdfc` (it fires on the genuine HDFC alert), `urgency` and `payment` on common words. The Hinglish, Hindi and Kannada cues (isolation and the others) were written by hand and not checked by a native speaker (`reports/spotcheck_*.csv` is still open). None of the lists was measured on val or test.
